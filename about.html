About This Guide

A short, independent directory for groups celebrating in Mount Pleasant, whether that's a bachelorette weekend, a milestone birthday, or a girls' night on Shem Creek.

Mount Pleasant has the waterfront bars but no strip clubs, and male revue nights around Charleston come and go, so most groups build the night around Shem Creek and a house or rental. This page covers what's actually available: the creek bars, the clubs across the bridge, touring shows, and private dancers who come to you.

Local performers we work with include Michael At Your Place, a Charleston-based entertainer who's a regular at Mount Pleasant bachelorette parties.

If you'd rather not plan it yourself, we can line up a private show at your place. The details are on the private party page. Everyone attending must be 18 or older.
