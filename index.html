Male Revues and Shows

Charleston doesn't have a dedicated male strip club, and male revue nights around the area come and go, so public shows take some planning. Touring revues like Magic Men Live and iCandy Male Revue, which plays Myrtle Beach and Darlington, come through the region a few times a year, and HonkyTonk Saloon in Ladson books an occasional revue or burlesque night, though that's a good 35 minutes up I-526. Most Mount Pleasant groups skip the drive and book a private dancer for the house.

Private Male Dancers

Michael At Your Place is a Charleston-based private dancer for bachelorette parties, birthdays, and girls' nights. Shows are $200 and run up to 90 minutes, with payment on arrival by cash, Venmo, or Cash App. He covers Charleston, Mount Pleasant, Folly Beach, and Isle of Palms, books both brunch and late-night shows, and welcomes last-minute dates.

Gentlemen's Clubs and Female Dancers

Mount Pleasant has no strip clubs, so groups head over the Ravenel Bridge. Downtown, King Street Cabaret, Secrets Cabaret, and Thee Southern Belle are all within about 20 minutes. North Charleston's G Club Gentlemen's Club and Gen X Cabaret are a bit farther, roughly 25 minutes up I-526. For the bachelor side of a wedding weekend, private female dancers can be booked for a house party through local entertainment services.

Shem Creek and Mount Pleasant Bars

Shem Creek is where most nights start. Red's Ice House has the big deck and sunset crowd, and Muddy's Dock Bar next door is a looser waterfront hang. Later on, Scotty Doesn't Know Speakeasy does cocktails behind an unmarked door, and The Commodore brings live music and a dance floor. Spending a beach day on the island?
